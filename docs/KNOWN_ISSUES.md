| 20 | Data | "Edit" on a recipe (the Recipes tab, the card's menu) replaces its use with a new one: the old use's contributions, ticks and decisions go and the edited lines come back unbought. So an edit saved after something of the recipe was bought unticks it, and after a finished trip puts the bought line back on the list as still needed. Pre-dates Release B (an edit has always been "the recipe again"), but it is the one path left on which a purchase is undone. A fix needs the new use to inherit the covered contributions and ticks of lines it keeps (matched by merge key, since the lines are stored afresh), or Edit to be withheld once something is bought. Found in the Release B bug hunt. Fixed: `ShoppingList.inheritPurchases` runs in `apply`'s transaction when a use is replaced — per ingredient what was bought stays bought on the row it was bought on (a finished row stays finished, the extra on the live row), the new amount is still needed only beyond it, fewer than were bought leaves the bought amount at the new one, and a dropped ingredient keeps its bought share with no line. | fixed |
| 21 | Backup | Undo after a restore (Settings › Data, 10 s) restores the whole database as it was before, so a tick on the phone or the watch, or a Finish shopping, in that window is undone with it, and the watch is forgotten a second time. Fixed: Undo is refused ("the list has changed since the restore") once `listRev` or the epoch has moved on from the restore's. | fixed |
| 22 | Sync | A snapshot page is keyed by `listRev` alone, but a page's content also depends on when it was built: past 16 pages, items ticked more than 30 minutes ago are left out (`SnapshotPager`), so a page 0 sent before that boundary and a page 1 sent after it can be assembled by the watch as one list with a row shifted.
