# Release C extraction evaluation — after the C.1 parser changes

Same 285 independent lines as the baseline (docs/reports/release-c-extraction-baseline.md),
same evaluator. Six labels were corrected against the spec between the two runs
(testdata/eval/release-c-2026-SOURCES.md › Label corrections); they account for at most
6 of the 285 lines (2 %), so the comparison below is the parser's, give or take that.

| | baseline (06e4126) | after C.1 |
|---|---|---|
| right (name, amount, unit) | 72 % | **88 %** |
| name | 74 % | 88 % |
| quantity | 91 % | 100 % |
| unit | 88 % | 99 % |
| catalogue match | 73 % | 87 % |
| aisle | 95 % | 98 % |
| shown amount | 81 % | 98 % |
| incorrectly accepted (confidently wrong) | 47 of 243 accepted (19 %) | **19 of 258 (7 %)** |
| flag rate (lines that would go to Claude when keyed) | 15 % | **9 %** |
| wrong readings caught by the flag | 32 of 79 | 15 of 34 |

On the frozen golden sets and the two earlier held-out sets (190 lines,
docs/reports/hybrid-gate.md) the kept lines went from 97 % / 98 % to 99 % / 98 % on
name / amount, and 2 more lines are kept rather than flagged (167 of 190).

## What the 19 remaining "incorrectly accepted" lines are

None is a wrong product or a wrong amount in the sense a shopper would notice; all are
differences between the label's name and the parser's on lines where the amount is right:

| kind | lines | example |
|---|---|---|
| a brand or origin word the parser keeps | rc-122, rc-131, rc-184, rc-211 | "King Arthur Unbleached All-Purpose Flour" |
| "fresh", "whole", "crushed", "boiling", "braising" kept or dropped the other way round | rc-017, rc-034, rc-162, rc-173, rc-191, rc-239 | "whole tomatoes" / "tomatoes" |
| a US word the label translated (canned → tinned, chili → chilli) | rc-184, rc-185, rc-266 | "canned tomato sauce" |
| "X cheese" against "X" | rc-211, rc-213 | "mascarpone cheese" |
| a product not in the catalogue (Nordic baking) | rc-201, rc-223, rc-224, rc-226 | "cracked rye kernels" |
| a parenthetical prep ("(minced…)") that the label folded into the name | rc-205 | "Beef (minced) 10.6 oz" → beef, not beef mince |
| a footnote marker and a hyphenated "or" | rc-260 | "long- or short-grain brown rice*" |

The 12 "incorrectly flagged" lines are flagged on purpose: nine "A or B" alternatives
(the review shows "Use B instead") and three measures the shop does not sell by
("rashers", "a small handful"), where the reading is right but worth a glance.

## Claude calls

Nothing here changes when Claude runs: a flagged line, page text, a photo or "Ask
Claude", as before. The flag rate on real pages fell from 15 % to 9 % of lines; since a
recipe goes to Claude when any of its lines is flagged, fewer recipes do. No live calls
were made for this evaluation (no key in the container; the recorded responses in
testdata/claude are unchanged).
