## Part 1 — Product and UX review

### Verdict

The app is coherent and unusually careful: the three journeys work end to end with one source of
truth each, every destructive action has Undo, the shopping screen is built for a hand in a
supermarket, and the W/X work fixed what a real weekly shopper hits first. The remaining weaknesses
are redundancy and friction rather than missing features: the same information appears in three
places; the review asks a newcomer to understand five states and buries the commonest correction
(the amount) under seven blocks; a slow Claude call holds the user on a spinner whose only exit
discards the import; the shop tick sits on the far side from a right thumb while a one-tap
"set aside" sits under it; Settings exposes engineering (voice test, Opus) in a product surface;
and a brand-new user lands on the planner with nothing to plan. The top ten improvements are
mostly small or medium and would make the app feel half as big while doing the same.

### Strengths

- The shop screen is a shop screen: pinned "5 left · Now: Produce" with a polite live region,
  52 dp ticks, ticked rows linger 2.5 s so a mis-tap is a second tap, bought rows fold per aisle,
  Next aisle in thumb reach, Finish never jumps (ShopScreen.kt:90-209, :141-174).
- "Not available" (X.8) is the right model: set aside for this trip, back next time, off the
  watch, never counted as left (ShopScreen.kt:514-531, WatchSyncCoordinator.kt:200).
- One meaning per concept, enforced in code and in CLAUDE.md: a planned meal is not a use, an
  amount is the sum of its contributions, ticks never create stock, purchases are never undone.
- Undo instead of dialogs, newest snackbar replacing the old, and refusals in plain words when
  Undo would be unsafe (ListRoute.kt:114-171; SettingsViewModel.kt:304).
- This week reads top to bottom as a week is prepared, with one bottom action whose subtitle says
  exactly what it will do (MealsRoute.kt:641-675).
- Buying-unit display: "1 × 30 g pack", "2 tins chopped tomatoes", half an onion is one onion,
  herbs in packs not millilitres.
- Accessibility designed in: roles, state descriptions, headings, 48–56 dp targets, 200 % font
  scale tests, nothing by colour alone, FLAG_SECURE while the key field is up.
- Privacy posture: Claude only from a foreground review, spend caps, the key out of every backup,
  wrong-reports shared only through the share sheet, no camera or Bluetooth permissions, two
  exported activities.

### Cut or simplify

1. Recipes › "Saved recipes / In this shop" toggle: make Recipes the recipe book only.
2. The "Plan this week's shop" card on the Shopping list (superseded by the tab).
3. Settings › Advanced › Voice test: a developer harness with a microphone foreground service;
   gate it to debug builds until voice mode exists.
4. Model choice Sonnet/Opus: evaluated on Haiku; 20–40× the cost against a $2 cap is a footgun.
5. "Group by type" view: since X.9 groups keep together everywhere; show group labels in Store
   order when an aisle has more than one group and drop the third view.
6. Four ways to change one line on the review sheet; also dead `watchVibrate` plumbing with no
   control rendered (SettingsRoute.kt:136, :551-560).

### What's missing (fits local-first, no backend)

1. Keep the screen on in shop mode (no `keepScreenOn` anywhere): a list that locks every 30 s is
   the first thing a shopper notices.
2. "Cooked it" on a meal: bumps times-cooked and prompts stars/notes; `LibraryOrder` admits it
   does not know what was cooked.
3. A carry-over prompt after Finish ("2 not bought: keep or drop?"), with improvement 7.
4. A second store's walking order, picked when the shop starts (pure local state, in the backup).
5. "Ran out" on a staple: one tap to list it once without un-stapling it.
6. Find on a long list in shop mode (a filter across aisles).
7. A launcher shortcut to Shopping with the Add field focused.
8. A one-time nudge to add a Claude key on the first offline-read review.

## Part 2 — Deep bug hunt

Severity: HIGH = data loss, a purchase undone or lost, a wrong amount on the list without a
flag, a crash, money; MEDIUM = wrong state or message, a dead end; LOW = narrow or cosmetic.
*Reproduced* = run against the compiled core library; otherwise traced through the real call path.

### A. Data layer and list model (ShoppingList, ListRepository, Planner, Backup)

1. **HIGH, reproduced — Edit over-asks when an ingredient was bought on two rows.**
   `ShoppingList.inheritPurchases` (ShoppingList.kt:473-510) computes the new use's shares once
   and loops per old row; with two bought rows for one key the second iteration re-reads the
   original share and overwrites the first's reduction. Sequence: "2 onions" → tick → Finish →
   servings 4→6 (1 extra on a new live row) → tick → Edit keeping "3 onions" → recipe asks 3,
   3 bought, list says "2 more". Caller: `ListRepository.apply(replacing=…)` ← `ReviewViewModel.confirm`.
   Fix: recompute the remaining need from the current contributions each iteration, or sum the
   bought rows per merge key before matching.

2. **HIGH, traced — A backup exported from a normal database can be refused on restore.**
   `ListRepository.persist` (:295-308) hard-deletes items that left the list but never removes
   their `DeletedItem` row (only `undelete` for rows whose `deletedAt` went null) and never deletes
   `ItemAlias` rows (`deleteAliases` is unused). `BackupValidator` then refuses: Backup.kt:207
   (`deleted N: item is missing`) and :138 (`alias … points at a missing item`). DeletedItem path:
   Clear all, then Undo of Buy again / "Have it" / unplan / remove-with-nothing-bought hard-deletes
   the row; self-heals only at the 7-day purge. Alias path is permanent: a catalogue re-key or
   "Swap for X" merges rows, the survivor is later emptied, the alias dangles for ever, and every
   export from then on fails to restore. Fix: in `persist`, after `deleteItems(gone)` also
   `undelete(gone)` and delete aliases touching `gone` (and prune `ShoppingList.aliases`); or have
   the exporter drop dangling rows. No test exports after a hard removal and restores.

3. **MEDIUM, reproduced — Edit after a finished trip leaves a "ghost" live row with nothing on it.**
   `inheritPurchases` (:500) removes the new share from the live row `merger.apply` just created
   when the new amount ≤ bought, but nothing removes the row (no `emptied` step as `rescaleUse`
   has at :587-596). "2 onions" → tick → Finish → Edit keeping "2 onions" → a row that is listed,
   counted as left, cannot be ticked (`tick()` returns null), survives Finish, and reaches the
   watch. Only Delete/Clear all clears it. Fix: drop touched live rows with no contributions.

4. **MEDIUM, reproduced — An ingredient the edit dropped comes back on a servings change.**
   The inherited no-line bought share is stored with `noScale = false` (:509); `rescaleUse`
   (:541-583) treats it as covered-and-growing and adds an open share. "2 onions, 1 carrot" →
   tick carrot → Edit to "2 onions" → servings 4→8 → "carrot: 1 bought, 1 more". Fix:
   `noScale = true` for the no-line share, or skip `recipeLineId == null` shares in `rescaleUse`.

5. **MEDIUM, reproduced (also found by the sync review) — A watch tick on a "Not available" item
   covers it but leaves it set aside.** `watchTick` (ShoppingList.kt:340) does not clear
   `unavailableAt` as `tick` (:241) does. Set aside on the phone, then a wrist tick from a
   snapshot sent before: done + set aside, excluded from `left`, deleted by Finish as bought
   instead of returning next trip; Undo of Finish brings it back still done and still set aside.
   Fix: clear `unavailableAt` in `watchTick` (or reject the op when unavailable).

6. **LOW — Shelf arrangement (X.9) is lost on any key change and never pruned.** `ShelfRank` keys
   embed aisle+group+mergeKey; `rekeyIfCatalogChanged` (ListRepository.kt:1270-1283), `swapItem`
   and `moveAisle` rename overrides and staples but not ranks. Fix: rename ranks alongside.

7. **LOW — The 7-day purge can remove a live use's bought share** (`purgeDeleted` :1213) when a
   use stays open across a week with its rescaled extra on a live row; progress then forgets the
   bought part. Awareness; needs a use open for a week.

8. **LOW — Two quick Arrange taps lose a move.** `moveItem/moveGroup` (ListViewModel.kt:493/505)
   read the cached order outside the write. Fix: read-move-write inside `arrange`.

9. **LOW — Validator gaps on a crafted file**: a `DeletedItem` row for a live item (the purge would
   hard-delete a live row), a contribution covered by another item's tick, `createdListRev > listRev`.

Coverage gaps: no invariant test runs an edit (`apply(replacing)`), `markUnavailable`, `swapItem`
or `editQuantity`, nor an export→restore after hard removals; the schema test that stops at v5.

### B. View models, UI and navigation

10. **MEDIUM (money) — A Claude answer paid for after "Stop" is thrown away, and "Ask Claude" pays
    again.** `stopRetry` (ReviewViewModel.kt:662) cancels only the waiter; the call completes in
    applicationScope and saves the CLAUDE result on the job, but the in-memory result stays the
    rules' parse and the next `persist()` (:423-434, on any tap) overwrites the saved answer;
    `retryWithClaude` (:624-626) then finds no in-flight call and pays again. Fix: on completion
    after Stop, lay the answer over (`keepCorrections` + store); and reuse a CLAUDE result already
    on the job before calling.

11. **MEDIUM (silent data loss) — System Back on an edit with changes discards without asking.**
    ReviewRoute.kt:104 `BackHandler(enabled = live && vm.isThrowaway) { vm.cancel() }` while the
    on-screen Cancel (:116) asks first when `changed`. Edit a saved recipe, rename a line, press
    Back: edits gone, no dialog, no Undo (suppressed for throwaways). Fix: same guard in the
    BackHandler.

12. **MEDIUM — "This week" goes stale across Sunday midnight.** `MealsViewModel` pins `current`
    at construction (:161-162) and the view model lives for the process; Monday morning the tab
    still shows last week as "This week", Buy again and At home attach to it, and "Add N meals"
    plans last week. `ListViewModel.prepFlow` already re-derives the week per list change. Fix:
    derive `current` inside the flow or on resume.

13. **MEDIUM — Library "Add to list" of a recipe already on the list asks twice, and dismissing
    the second prompt leaves a phantom "1 recipe ready to review".** RecipesRoute.kt:216/:345 ask,
    then `ReviewViewModel.load()` asks again (AlreadyOnList); ReviewRoute.kt:193
    `onDismissRequest = onDone` leaves the throwaway job READY on the Shopping tab's ready card
    until "Don't add" or a restart. Fix: for a throwaway AlreadyOnList, dismiss = cancel; drop the
    first dialog.

14. **MEDIUM-LOW — Finish shopping with "Not available" rows stays in shop mode with them as
    "left".** Only an empty list switches SHOP→PLAN (ListViewModel.kt:461-468). Same root as
    UX improvement 7. Fix: Finish sets PLAN.

15. **MEDIUM-LOW — Any edit in the review row sheet resets every other line's draft.** Editors
    are keyed by `recipeLineId` (ReviewRowSheet.kt:270; Components.kt:369) and `replaceLines`
    re-stores the recipe with new line ids. Fix: key by position within the row or `LineOrigin`.

16. **LOW — aisleInStock/clearInStock Undo restores the whole decisions map** (:586, :598): a
    toggle made after the aisle action is reverted by its Undo. Fix: capture only the changed keys.

17. **LOW — Add-item duplicate check reads a stale list** (ListViewModel.kt:708 `lastList`): two
    quick Enters of the same item add two contributions instead of "already on the list". Fix:
    `repository.snapshot()`.

18. **LOW — A review opened over Settings › Library pops Settings too** (HyperShopApp.kt:266
    `popBackStack(LIST, false)`). Fix: pop to the nearest tab.

19. **LOW — Lingering tick makes the aisle pill disagree with the top bar for 2.5 s**
    (ShopScreen.kt:382 counts the lingering row as open). Fix: count by state, not by group.

20. **LOW — Transient-failure "Try again" can navigate after the user has left**
    (ReviewRoute.kt:149). Guard with a route check as `onUndo` does.

### C. Extraction, display and Claude

21. **MEDIUM-HIGH, reproduced — List-marker offsets corrupt notes, lose "(optional)" and can glue
    the name.** `withoutMarker` (RuleParser.kt:50) tokenises the marker-stripped string while
    `span()` (:759), `spacedDash()` (:735) and the "(optional)" check (:498-500) index the original
    text, so every offset is shifted by the marker's length. "- 1 egg (optional)" → not optional,
    note "(option"; "• 1 onion, finely chopped" → note ", finely chopp"; "- 100 g sugar (plus 50g
    for dusting)" → quantity text "- 1"; "- cheddar - big block" → name "cheddar-block", unflagged.
    Every pasted bulleted list is hit. Fix: blank the marker to spaces instead of removing it.

22. **MEDIUM, reproduced — "1-inch piece ginger" → item "inch piece ginger", qty 1, unflagged**
    (RuleParser.kt:147-155 handles `NUM lengths`, not `NUM DASH lengths`). Never merges with ginger.

23. **MEDIUM, reproduced — "juice from 1 lemon" / "zest from 1 orange" → item "juice"/"zest",
    no amount, unflagged** ("from" is a name-ender at :568 before `juiceOf` runs; "juice of" works).

24. **MEDIUM, reproduced — Review rows show "10 packs basil", "2 jars bay leaves", "4 jars
    cardamom pods"** for counted presence items: `Display.brief` (Display.kt:218-219) falls through
    to `packs()` (:326-334), which uses the ITEMS count as the pack count; the list row shows only
    the name, so review and list disagree. Used at ReviewViewModel.kt:558 and ListViewModel.kt:394.

25. **MEDIUM-LOW, reproduced — A presence item measured by volume with no density can come out
    short**: honey 2 cups → "1 jar" (454 g jar, ~680 g needed); maple syrup 1 cup → "1 bottle"
    (250 g). Display.kt:154-163 falls back to 1 for PRESENCE even when the pack size is known.

26. **MEDIUM-LOW — "1 tin beans" → dried beans (Pasta, rice & grains), unflagged**: the catalogue's
    bare "beans" synonym and `Shelf.of` (Shelf.kt:158) re-shelving only PRODUCE for a tin.

27. **MEDIUM-LOW — Trailing amounts have no plausibility check**: pasted recipe text yields
    unflagged junk rows ("Serves 4" → serves ×4, "Preheat oven to 200" → preheat oven ×200)
    (RuleParser.kt:72-114). Fix: flag a bare trailing number on a non-catalogue name, or cap it.

28. **LOW-MEDIUM, reproduced — Division by zero crash**: RuleParser.kt:273 and :320 compute
    `altQty × (qty + more) / qty` with a literal 0 ("0 cups (0g) plus 1 cup flour"). Via
    `ReviewViewModel.useLines` the exception reaches viewModelScope and crashes the app; via the
    worker it becomes NO_RECIPE. Fix: guard `qty.isZero`.

29. **LOW-MEDIUM — "7g fresh yeast" files as fast-action dried yeast** with "fresh" in the note.

30. **LOW — "500g lean minced beef" stays an uncatalogued "lean minced beef"** and never merges with
    beef mince (RuleParser.kt:541-575). Add synonyms or move the unknown leading word to the note.

31. **LOW — Onion 910 g → 7 onions** (rounds up from 6.07; the 10 % slack applies only to packs);
    the KDoc at Catalog.kt:29 says six.

32. **LOW — Usage under-recorded in two narrow paths**: an SSE `error` after `message_start` drops
    the known input tokens before the retry (ClaudeClient.kt:308-319); a complete 200 that lands
    after the caller was cancelled is not spilled (:184-189, a race).

33. **LOW — "pkg"/"pkgs" is not a PACKET spelling** (Lexicon.kt:338): "1 pkg cream cheese" → name
    "pkg cream cheese", unflagged.

### D. Watch sync, backup, files and security

34. **HIGH, traced — Wrist ticks can be silently dropped until the next epoch.** An unsolicited
    push targets `install ?: repository.lastInstall()` (WatchSyncCoordinator.kt:146), but
    `lastInstall()` is `ORDER BY rowid DESC` on an INTEGER PRIMARY KEY (ListDao.kt:323), i.e. the
    largest install id, not the newest; the page's `at` is that install's `lastSeq`; the watch
    settles every outbox op with `seq <= acked` with no check that it issued them
    (WatchSync.mc:286, :362-386; WatchSyncModel.kt:130-142); and the next hello fast-forwards the
    phone with `maxOf(lastSeq, hello.at)` (ListRepository.kt:1301). Sequence: the watch app is
    reinstalled (new random install id; the old row stays until an epoch change; 50 % it is
    smaller), the phone restarts with a tick in flight → the tick is settled "ok" without being
    applied, and every later op reads as a duplicate: acked, never applied. The same with two
    paired watches. Fix: watch treats `acked >= nextSeq` as foreign (do not settle, hello again);
    phone pushes `ackedThrough = 0` and no `ax` to an install that has not spoken this process;
    give `WatchInstall` a last-seen stamp.

35. **MEDIUM — Watch tick on a set-aside item**: same as A.5.

36. **LOW — 64-bit ids and listRev truncated to 32-bit on the wire; the backup validator has no
    upper bounds** (WatchSyncCoordinator.kt:212-223, ListRepository.kt:1328, Backup.kt:99-120). A
    crafted backup with ids ≥ 2^31 makes every batch unreadable and the watch resend for ever;
    a hello with `at = Int.MAX_VALUE` overflows `last + 1`. Fix: cap in the validator; leave off
    items that do not fit.

37. **LOW — `BackupManager.inspect` holds up to 64 MB and parses it twice** (BackupManager.kt:242-299,
    :448); an OutOfMemoryError on a 40–64 MB file is not caught. Fix: a tiny header decode, one
    parse, a 16 MB cap.
