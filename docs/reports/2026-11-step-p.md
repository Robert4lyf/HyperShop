sub-step, each with `PROGRESS.md`. No database schema, backup format, package id, signing, watch app id or watch

|---|---|---|

Defects found on the way and fixed in P.4: a request waiting at a cold start (the launcher shortcut's too) navigated
before `NavHost` had its graph, which throws; and Add resumed off the main thread after writing the list mode, then
navigated. Both now wait for the first destination on the main thread. In P.2, the new dialog first never settled its
layout under test; it now uses the full-width pattern N.8 introduced for the home-amount dialog.

## Interpretation results, kept apart

**Regression results (not independent).** The November shopping set (20 model-authored lines) was used to choose
the two changes, so it is now a regression set. Rule parser only. Labels unchanged.

| | before (34898f0) | after |
|---|---|---|
| Label mismatches | 5 | 3 |
| Unflagged label mismatches | 4 | 2 |
| Unflagged identity/quantity mismatches | 0 | 0 |
| Unflagged ingredient-part losses | (not reported) 1 | 0 |
| Unflagged consequential-qualifier losses | (not reported) 1 | 0 |

The remaining mismatches are "half a cucumber", "a splash of milk" (review judgments still awaiting a person) and
"a 300 g bag of spinach" (flagged, identity wrong). Neighbouring cases are pinned by `MeaningTest`: "2 eggs plus 2 egg
whites", "1 egg, plus 1 extra yolk", "2 egg yolks plus 1 egg" flagged; "1 egg plus 1 egg", "2 egg yolks",
"2 egg yolks plus 1 egg yolk", "100g white sugar plus 50g white sugar" not; heaped/heaping/rounded/generous spoons and
cups flagged; level, scant, "a generous pinch" and plain same-measure arithmetic ("2 tbsp plus 1 tsp") not. These
neighbouring lines were written alongside the change by the same author, so they are regression checks too.

**Effect on the labelled held-out sets (recorded, previously used).** Exactly four lines gained a flag, amounts
unchanged: fq-069 "1 heaped tsp hot chilli powder…", rc-115 "1 rounded tsp dried fenugreek leaves…", tq-017
"1 heaped tbsp light brown soft sugar", tq-047 "1 large egg, plus 1 egg yolk". Every floor holds (right, name,
quantity, unit, catalogue, shown, accepted-wrong). On Claude's recorded readings, right and accepted-wrong are
unchanged and right-but-flagged rises by one in each set (t 0→1, final 1→2, release C 2→3). That is the cost: a
person is asked to look at a heaped spoon that was read at the level amount. The baseline files changed only by
those four `F` marks (`HYPERSHOP_EVAL_UPDATE=1`, reviewed line by line).

**Fresh evaluation: none.** No set of lines unseen by this change was scored. No live Claude call was made.

**Real-user performance: unmeasured.** Nothing here says how often real recipes lose meaning or how long review takes.

2. Recipes: 10 complete recipes not used in any set here (5 web links from different sites, 3 pasted texts, 2 cookbook

4. Clock starts at the share or paste confirmation; stops when the person, looking at the shopping list, says it would

5. Count each correction made on review: an amount set, a name or swap, a line edited, removed or added, an at-home

6. Note any wrong item that reached the list unflagged, by kind: identity, amount, ingredient part, qualifier.
7. Report per recipe and median: corrections, unnecessary checks, unflagged errors, seconds, and the Claude cost.
   Three people if possible; one person's numbers are reported as one person's.
