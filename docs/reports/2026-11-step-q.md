## The two journeys, measured

Measured by `JourneyTest` through the real app shell (`HyperShopApp`, Robolectric). A tap is a click or a keyboard
action, including the tap that focuses a field. A screen change is a new destination or a dialog/sheet opening.
These are counts, not timings, and no person took part.

| Journey | Before (93d99fa) | After |
|---|---|---|
| Quick shopping: first run, type milk and bread, tick both, finish | 7 taps · 2 typed · 0 scrolls · 0 screen changes | 7 · 2 · 0 · 0 (unchanged) |
| Meal-based: three saved recipes, one served to 5, put on the list, rice partly at home, ready to shop | 9 taps · 1 typed · 5 scrolls · 2 screen changes | 13 taps · 1 typed · 5 scrolls · 6 screen changes |

The meal journey now takes four more taps and four more screen changes:

- opening and closing the Add meal sheet;
- opening and leaving the food-at-home screen.

The old journey was shorter only because everything sat on one long screen at once. Whether the new version is
easier to understand is a question for people using it, and nobody has tested it yet. The food-at-home part is
optional. Without it, the after journey is 8 taps and 3 screen changes to reach the list.

## Not done, and why

- **Editing the amount still to buy.** The sheet leads with "Buy N", and the editor is labelled as editing the total
  needed. Changing the buying amount would need a new repository operation that maps through covered
  contributions, home allocations and mixed measures. A subtraction would be wrong, and the brief rules it out.
- **"Have some at home" from the item sheet.** This is available per ingredient on Food at home, which is reached
  from the list. A per-item path would need the same allocation rules applied to a single item. That operation
  doesn't exist yet.
- **Sharpening the review for corrected recipes.** Reusing a saved recipe no longer opens the review at all. A new
  import's review still lists every line; flagged lines already show their reason and source text (step P).

## Verification limits

There was no device, emulator, launcher, TalkBack or Forerunner in this container. Screenshots are Robolectric
renders, inspected for light, dark and 2× text. The emulator device scenarios (`WeeklyShopScenariosTest`,
`DeviceScenariosTest`) follow the new flow. Picks are made in the Add meal sheet, Buy again and food at home are
reached from the list, and Shopping view is opened from the list. These scenarios compile here and run only in CI.
Scenario B's quick-way tap budget was raised from 6 to 10, to match the measured new path. That is the cost of the
sheet and the screen the brief asked for, and it is stated here and in the test rather than hidden.
